## What shipped

Frontend-only patch. The AI settings page (`/settings/ai`) was stuck
showing "Loading AI settings..." forever for any user whose
`company_id` was null — typical for anyone who registered publicly and
hadn't yet joined or created a company.

Root cause: `loadConfig()` returned early when `companyId` was falsy
without clearing the `loading` flag, and the initial `loading` state is
`true`. No error was logged anywhere — the backend returned 200 on
every call, the page just never exited the loading state.
